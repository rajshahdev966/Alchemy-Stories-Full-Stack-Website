Author Guide: How to Add & Write Blog Entries

Follow these quick architectural steps to register a new entry in the Natural Journal catalog:

01
Add to Database

Open blog-data.js. Append a new object containing: unique id, title, category, author, date, readTime, cover image, description, and a content array of paragraph strings.

02
Link Card in Index

In journal.html, change the card links from general # to blog.html?id=YOUR_ID, matching the database key. They will resolve automatically.

03
Deploy & Test

Refresh your browser and verify the new card redirect. The dynamic engine will immediately read your custom coordinates, images, and paragraphs without compiling any files!
