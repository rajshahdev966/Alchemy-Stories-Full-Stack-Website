## SECTION 04 — THE ALCHEMY PROCESS

### Layout

Sticky Horizontal Scroll Experience

As the user scrolls:

Each step

↓

SVG Illustration lights up

↓

Short explanation appears

↓

Three process photographs reveal

↓

Photographs moves back in the SVG

↓

Scroll continues

↓

Next step activates

The next step should never reveal until the current step finishes.
