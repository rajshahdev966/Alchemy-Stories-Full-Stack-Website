Behind the Imprints

Preserving ephemeral moments of the natural world.

We do not simply produce artwork; we catalog moments. Our founders Reena Jasani & Kushan Jasani bridge traditional botanical collecting techniques with contemporary solar-developing printmaking.

Using sunlight, water, and organic specimens collected from forest floors and riverbanks, each piece holds the memory of the exact date, weather, and location of its exposure.
