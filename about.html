Studio Overview

Our Story

We link botanical collections with solar chemistry to design custom home installations.

The Genesis

Reena Jasani & Kushan Jasani

Alchemy Stories began in 2014 as a research experiment. Rooted in Ahmedabad, we sought to bridge the gap between traditional botanical preservation and premium interior design.

Reena Jasani, textile designer and curator, guides the specimen selection process. Kushan Jasani manages chemistry developments and handcrafts our signature Teakwood frames in our local workshop. Together, they direct an team of local craftspeople.

12+ Years Curation
1,200+ Archived Prints
Atelier Workshop

Our Ahmedabad Studio

Our studio houses botanical storage, a custom chemical darkroom, and an adjacent woodshop.

Every step of printmaking takes place under one roof. We harvest leaves from our studio garden, mix iron compounds by hand, expose papers under natural sunlight on our terrace, and wash prints in filtered rainwater collectors.

Timeline

Our Journey

2014

Genesis of the Studio

Initial chemistry trials exposing local Sabarmati riverbed foliage on hand-sensitized watercolor papers. Establishing our first darkroom layout.

2017

First Museum Exhibition

Exhibited 'Prussian Solitude' collection at the Jehangir Art Gallery in Mumbai, introducing scientific herbaria layouts to fine interior designers.

2021

Bespoke Commissions Launch

Partnered with luxury interior architecture firms across India to deliver custom hotel panels and botanical lighting systems.

2026

Bespoke Digital Gallery

Opening our updated interactive exhibition catalog, supporting direct purchases, custom story uploads, and catalog searches.
