Details about the printmaking methodology and natural elements.

We source unbleached, hand-crafted Khadi and French cotton rag papers to guarantee long-term fibers stability. Sensitizing solutions are hand-coated under darkroom guidelines using refined iron compounds (Ferric Ammonium Citrate and Potassium Ferricyanide).

Cyanotype prints are chemically stable but can fade if exposed to continuous direct midday UV sunlight. We apply a protective museum sealer; however, we advise framing with UV-protective glass and hanging prints away from highly damp walls or direct sun spots.
