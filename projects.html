Custom Projects

We collaborate with interior architects and private collectors to design site-specific botanical compositions.

Case Study 01

Jaipur Palace Lobby Panel

An architectural installation consisting of six 8-foot custom-coated linen canvas screens, designed for the grand entrance atrium of a heritage boutique resort.

Challenge: Incorporating indigenous botanical textures into a large-scale wall installation that connects structure with landscaping.
Solution: Collected wild desert grasses and thistles native to Rajasthan. Exposed on sensitized unbleached linen canvases using direct winter solar rays.
Location Jaipur, Rajasthan
Date Completed February 2026
Case Study 02

Ahmedabad Minimalist Residence

Designing a custom collection of pressed Ginkgo leaf panel frames and organic lighting fixtures for a modern, minimal double-height lounge.

Challenge: Bringing warmth and subtle natural movement to solid exposed-concrete architectural walls.
Solution: Coordinated four matching Ginkgo triptychs and custom-fabricated two 6-foot tall Teakwood Tower Lamps lined with hand-coated blueprint screens.
Location Ahmedabad, Gujarat
Date Completed April 2026
