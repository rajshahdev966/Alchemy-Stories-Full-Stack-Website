Yes, we ship safely across India and worldwide. Unframed prints are rolled and shipped in reinforced shipping tubes. Framed commissions are securely packed in custom wooden crates to prevent glass cracks.

Custom commissions typically take 3 to 4 weeks. This includes the initial foliage press drying, solar exposure scheduling (dependent on seasonal sunlight), chemical washes, hand-signature drying, and wooden frame molding in our studio.

Prussian Blue is chemically stable and durable. However, direct mid-day UV exposure over several years can cause mild fading. If faded, placing the print in a dark drawer for a few days initiates chemical reduction, restoring its rich indigo blue tone. We frame prints under UV-protective museum glass to prevent fading.
